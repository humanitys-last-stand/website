About

The world has been taken by the storm of AI, and while we see a growing list of reasons to embrace it - we have chosen to do the opposite. Humanity’s Last Stand is a small indie game studio focussed on building games that we want to play, without using Generative AI. We have purposesly made it part of our creed to abandon nearly all usages of AI. We know that this may come at the sacrifice of speed - but that’s the thing.

We really enjoy crafting things ourselves.

We want the joy of creating something out of nothing and favor the right to claim all of the craftsmanship as our own. We want to make a game that you know was made for humans, by humans, with love. We have no hate or judgment for those who do leverage AI in any shape - it’s just a fundamental choice we’ve made for ourselves.
